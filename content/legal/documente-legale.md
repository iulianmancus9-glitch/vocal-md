## Refund Policy

**Effective date: 13 September 2026**

### The basic principle

We give you the lyrics and roughly one minute of your song, in two interpretations, **before** asking for any money. You pay only if you liked what you heard.

For this reason, once payment is made, **the sale is final**. The song is created uniquely for you and cannot be reused or resold.

### When we do refund

We issue a full refund or provide a replacement in the following cases:

**Non-delivery.** You did not receive the complete song because of a technical error on our side, and we are unable to deliver it even after you report the problem.

**Faulty file.** The audio file delivered is corrupted, empty or unplayable, and we cannot provide a working replacement.

**Wrong song.** You received a different song from the one you ordered and approved.

**Duplicate charge.** You were charged twice for the same order. We refund the extra amount.

**Order refused by us.** We cancelled your order because we could not process it. In that case the refund is full and automatic.

### When we do not refund

- The song was delivered correctly but you do not like the style, the voice or the interpretation — the free preview exists precisely so you can judge this before paying.
- You mistyped a name, a date or a detail of your story. Please check the summary before approving the lyrics.
- You changed your mind about the recipient or the occasion after purchase.
- The pronunciation of a name is not exactly as you wished, but the track otherwise matches your order.

### How to request a refund

Write to **base.vocalmd@gmail.com** within **14 days** of your purchase, quoting the email address used for the order and describing the problem.

We reply within 5 working days. Approved refunds are processed by Lemon Squeezy and usually reach your account within 5–10 working days, depending on your bank.

### Refunds through Lemon Squeezy

Because **Lemon Squeezy (Link, LLC)** is the Merchant of Record for our sales, refunds are issued through their system. You may also contact Lemon Squeezy support directly using the details on the receipt you received.
